01 — Studio

Architecture with a sense of place.

AURA Studio approaches every project as an opportunity to create spaces that feel considered, comfortable and connected to the people who use them.

We begin with light and how it moves through a day, then choose materials that age well and proportions that feel natural to inhabit. Each decision is tested against its context — the site, the climate, the street — and against everyday life: how a home is really used, from the first coffee to the last light.
