VoiceFormAI

Computer Vision / Voice / Automation

Combines document understanding, OCR, and computer vision to identify form fields on a paper form and match them with spoken answers, so a person can fill a form by talking instead of writing by hand.

Technology
Python · YOLO · OpenCV · OCR · Gemini / Vision

Key Features

  • Detects and locates writable fields on a scanned or photographed form.
  • Converts spoken answers into text mapped to the correct field.
  • Uses OCR and vision models together to handle real, imperfect paper forms.
